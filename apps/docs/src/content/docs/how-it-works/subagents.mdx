A subagent is the bot itself, at work on a part of a job in a thread of its own. A bot asked to
compare five hotels can start five subagents, one per hotel, and bring what they find together. To
give work to a *different* bot, a bot messages it instead (see [Messages between
bots](/how-it-works/messages-between-bots/)).

<BotThreads caption="Subagents and automation runs work in threads beneath the bot's conversation and report back into it. A message to another bot goes into that bot's own conversation." />

## How they work

- **Starting one.** The bot calls `spawn_subagent` with the task and a short title, and can give it
  another model. The subagent starts at once, in a thread beneath the conversation, with the bot's
  instructions, files, sign-ins and superpowers, and a browser window of its own.
- **What it is told.** The task, and that it is a subagent. It sees nothing of the conversation, so
  the task has to say everything it needs.
- **Coming back.** When a subagent is done, what it came to arrives in the parent's thread as a
  report, which the bot reads in its turn like any message. A bot that has nothing else to do can
  wait for its subagents with `wait_subagents` instead; **Send now** on a message of yours cuts
  that wait short.
- **Looking in.** The bot can list its subagents, read what one has done so far, write to one
  that is still at work, or stop it (`list_subagents`, `read_subagent`, `message_subagent`,
  `cancel_subagent`). So can you: open one from beneath the conversation to watch it live, write
  to it, or stop it.
- **Stopping.** Stopping a bot stops every subagent beneath it.

## Limits

| Limit | Default | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subagents a thread can have at work at once | 8 (1 to 16) | **Settings → Advanced → Subagents at once** |
| Levels of subagents beneath a conversation | 2 (0 to 3) | **Settings → Advanced → Subagent levels** |

A subagent at the last level has no subagent tools at all. A run of an automation counts as one
level, so its subagents are one level down.

A subagent cannot message other bots or make bots: only the conversation itself talks to other
bots. It can ask you to take over its browser window, like the conversation can. Its window
closes a minute after it finishes, and its thread is kept.

<Aside>
  A subagent shares everything with the bot: the same folder, the same sign-ins, the same
  superpowers. It is a way to work in parallel, not a way to give part of a job less power. To keep
  a kind of work apart, give it a bot of its own.
</Aside>
