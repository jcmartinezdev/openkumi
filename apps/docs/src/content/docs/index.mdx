OpenKumi is a free, open-source desktop app for AI bots that live on your computer. Each bot has
its own memory, its own signed-in browser and only the powers you give it, and it runs on the
Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan you already have, or on a local model.

There is no account to make and no server of ours in between. Everything a bot is and knows is a
folder of plain files on a computer of yours.

<BigPicture caption="The app is only a window. The server does the work, on the computer you choose, and reaches your AI plan, the web and the apps you sign the bots in to." />
