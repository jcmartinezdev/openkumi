In the desktop app, **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) opens the search from anywhere in the
window: halfway through a message, in Settings, even while a bot's browser has the keyboard.
**Search** at the top of the sidebar and **View → Search…** in the menu bar open it too. Press ⌘K
again, Esc, or click outside it to close it.

## What it finds

Type part of a name. Every word you type has to be there, and names that start with what you typed
come first.

- **Bots**, by name or by what they are for. Enter opens the bot's conversation.
- **The chat you're in**: call the bot, start fresh, its earlier conversations, its browser, its
  model, sending files.
- **Places**: Automations, Usage, Settings and each of its pages.
- **Settings**: every setting the search in Settings finds. Enter opens its page, with the setting
  picked out. Nothing is changed from the search itself.
- **Superpowers**, **automations**, and **conversations** (earlier ones, subagents, and the runs
  of automations), by name.
- **In messages**: words that you or a bot wrote, in every bot's conversations. They come a moment
  after you stop typing, and Enter opens the conversation at that message.
- **Show another person's bots…**, when more than one person or computer is set up.

With nothing typed it lists your bots with their marks (**Needs you**, **Read this**), what you can
do in the chat you're in, and what you picked lately. Start with `>` to see only things to do.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ↑ ↓ | Move through the list |
| Enter | Open what is picked, or do it |
| Tab | Open up the bot that is picked: everything you can do with it, its automations, superpowers and conversations. Typing then searches only that bot. |
| ⌫ | With nothing typed, go back out |
| Esc | Close the search |

The bar along the bottom of the search shows the keys for what is picked.

## Your first nine bots

**⌘1** to **⌘9** (Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9) open your first nine bots, in the order the sidebar lists
them, from anywhere in the window. Hold **⌘** (Ctrl) for a moment and each bot in the sidebar shows
its number. If the sidebar lists the bot you last talked with first, the numbers follow that order
and change as you talk.
