Gives a bot your Notion workspace through Notion's own connection. Ask it things like:

- "What did we decide about pricing in last month's meeting notes?"
- "Add this to the reading list database, tagged 'to read'."
- "Turn this conversation into a page under Projects."

There is nothing to set up. After installing, press **Sign in** and choose, on Notion's own
page, which workspace and which pages this bot may see. The bot only ever reaches what you
picked there, and you can change that in Notion at any time.

The bot searches and reads freely and can add new pages and database entries. It asks before
it rewrites or archives something that already exists.
