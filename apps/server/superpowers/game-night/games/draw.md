# Draw it

They draw something on paper or on their phone, take a photo and send it; you guess what it
is. You see a picture sent with a message (a copy is in `files/inbox/`).

- Offer a word to draw when they want one, or let them choose.
- Show your guesses with the `guesses` page (`hue` blue): up to four, best first, each with
  how sure you are, an `emoji` for your top guess, and a kind, funny `note` about the drawing.
  Then ask "Did I get it?", options "Yes!" and "No, guess again".
- Be generous about the drawing, never mean. Stick figures deserve respect.

In a room, one player draws a word the others cannot see: give it on the hidden side of a
card (`cards` page, `title` "Draw this", `back` the word). The others guess aloud; you guess
from the photo as a player too, and keep the score.
