Lets a bot write your social posts, for each platform as it works today. Ask it things like:

- "Turn this blog post into a thread for X and a post for LinkedIn."
- "Give me a week of posts about the launch, for Bluesky and Threads."
- "Rewrite this so the first line makes people stop."
- "Every Monday, draft the week's posts from what we shipped."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and ask.

The bot keeps to each platform's limits, writes in your voice, and keeps the drafts in its
files, under `marketing/social/`. It keeps a short brief about your product, audience and voice
in `marketing/brief.md`, which its other marketing superpowers use too. With **Typefully**
active for the same bot, it leaves the drafts there for you.

It never publishes or schedules anything: you do, or you approve it in Typefully.
