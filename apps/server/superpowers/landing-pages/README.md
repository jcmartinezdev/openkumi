Lets a bot review the pages that are meant to turn visitors into customers, and rewrite them.
Ask it things like:

- "Why isn't the home page getting sign-ups? Here's the link."
- "Rewrite the pricing page so the Pro plan is easier to choose."
- "Give me three headlines for the launch page, each from a different angle."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and give it a link.

The bot opens the page in its own browser, ranks what to fix, and shows the new copy laid out
like the page, beside the old. It keeps a short brief about your product, audience and voice
in its files, under `marketing/brief.md`, which its other marketing superpowers use too.

It never changes your site, and never makes up numbers, customers or quotes: where proof is
missing, it leaves a gap for you to fill.
