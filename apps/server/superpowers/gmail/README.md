Lets a bot work in your Gmail, through Google's own service. Ask it things like:

- "What came in since yesterday that I actually need to answer?"
- "Find the invoice Marta sent in March and tell me the total."
- "Draft a reply to the landlord saying the 14th works."

The bot searches and reads freely, and writes drafts. It cannot send anything: a draft waits in
Gmail until you send it yourself. It asks before it labels, archives, trashes or marks anything
as spam. As it works with you it notes how you write and who your regular contacts are, so its
drafts get closer to yours over time.

Open Bot has to be registered with Google once, for all the Google superpowers; this page walks
you through it. After that, activate it for a bot and sign in to Google in the bot's browser,
with the account that bot should use.
