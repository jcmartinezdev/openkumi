Lets a bot work on how your site is found: on Google, and in the answers of AI assistants such
as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. Ask it things like:

- "Audit openkumi.ai and tell me what to fix first."
- "Can ChatGPT and Claude read our site at all?"
- "Rewrite the pricing page so it answers what people ask."
- "Check the site again every month and tell me what got worse."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and give it your
site.

The bot reads your site three ways: as plain HTML, the way most AI crawlers read it; in its own
browser, the way Google sees it; and the raw files, for robots.txt and the sitemap. It saves
each audit in its files, under `marketing/seo/`, and keeps a short brief about your product in
`marketing/brief.md`, which its other marketing superpowers use too.

For real search figures, export the Performance report from Google Search Console and attach
it to a message. The bot never changes your site: it tells you, or your developer, what to
change.
