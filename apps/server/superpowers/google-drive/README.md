Lets a bot work in your Google Drive, through Google's own service. Ask it things like:

- "Find last year's tax documents and tell me what is still missing."
- "What does the onboarding guide say about expenses?"
- "Save this summary as a new document in Drive."

The bot searches and reads everything your account can see, and can create, upload and copy
files. It can change only files it made itself: Google keeps your other files out of its
reach, and it never shares or deletes anything.

OpenKumi has to be registered with Google once, for all the Google superpowers; this page walks
you through it. After that, activate it for a bot and sign in to Google in the bot's browser,
with the account that bot should use.
