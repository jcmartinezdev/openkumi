Lets a bot look into your apps' errors in Sentry, through Sentry's own service. Ask it things
like:

- "What are the top new errors in the web app since yesterday's release?"
- "Why is checkout failing for some users? Show me the stack trace."
- "Which issues are assigned to me and still unresolved?"

There is nothing to set up. Activate it for a bot, press **Sign in**, and approve it on Sentry's
own page. There you tick what the bot may do: look into issues, ask Seer, triage issues, and
manage projects and alerts; untick what you would rather keep to yourself. It works on every
Sentry plan, including the free one; Seer needs a paid plan. Self-hosted Sentry is not reachable.

The bot reads freely. It asks before it resolves, assigns or comments on an issue, or changes a
project or alert.
