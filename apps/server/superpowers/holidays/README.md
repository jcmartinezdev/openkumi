Gives a bot the public holidays of about 200 countries, from the free
[Nager.Date](https://date.nager.at/) service. Ask it things like:

- "Which long weekends are there in Spain next year, and which days should I take off?"
- "Is anything closed in Germany the week I'm there?"
- "Find a day for the call that isn't a holiday in the US, the UK or India."
- "Skip my morning brief on public holidays."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and ask.

The holidays are kept by Nager.Date's volunteers. For a date that matters, such as a deadline
or a bank closing, the bot checks it with an official source too.
