Lets a bot work in your Attio CRM, through Attio's own service. Ask it things like:

- "Who at Acme have we talked to, and when was the last time?"
- "Add the three people from today's call and put them on the Q1 pipeline."
- "Which deals haven't moved in two weeks?"

There is nothing to set up. Activate it for a bot, press **Sign in**, and choose a workspace on
Attio's own page; to switch workspaces later, sign in again. It works on every Attio plan, and
the bot sees what your Attio account sees.

The bot reads freely and can add records, notes and tasks. It asks before it changes or merges a
record you did not tell it to. It cannot delete records or send email.
