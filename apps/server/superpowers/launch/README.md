Lets a bot plan a launch with you, and keep it on track. Ask it things like:

- "We launch the iPhone app in three weeks. Make the plan."
- "Write the Show HN post and the first comment."
- "Is this ready for Product Hunt, or will it be turned away?"
- "How did yesterday's launch go?"

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and ask.

The bot knows the current rules of Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit and the main launch
directories, and drafts every post and email for them. It keeps the plan in its files, under
`marketing/launch/`, reminds you of each step on the day, and gathers how it went. It keeps a
short brief about your product in `marketing/brief.md`, which its other marketing superpowers
use too.

It never posts, submits, votes or messages anyone: you do, from your own accounts.
