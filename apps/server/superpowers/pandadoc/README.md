Lets a bot work on your PandaDoc documents, through PandaDoc's own service. Ask it things like:

- "Which proposals are still waiting for a signature?"
- "Draft a quote for Acme from the Standard template, for 20 seats."
- "Has the Lisbon lease been signed, and by whom?"

Activating it signs the bot in: you choose a workspace on
PandaDoc's own page. It reaches accounts in PandaDoc's US region; an account in the EU region
cannot sign in here.

The bot reads freely and drafts documents for you to check. It asks before it sends a document
for signature or approval, or changes a document's status.
