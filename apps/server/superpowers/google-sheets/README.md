Lets a bot work in your Google Sheets, through Google's own service. Ask it things like:

- "What did we spend on travel in Q3?" (with a link to the budget)
- "Add today's three orders to the tracker."
- "Add a column that works out the margin for each row."

The bot reads and adds to the spreadsheets you point it to: values, formulas, rows and columns.
It asks before it overwrites or deletes anything already there. To find spreadsheets by name,
also activate Google Drive for the bot; otherwise give it a link.

OpenKumi has to be registered with Google once, for all the Google superpowers; this page walks
you through it. After that, activate it for a bot and sign in to Google in the bot's browser,
with the account that bot should use.
