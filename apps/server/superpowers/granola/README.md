Lets a bot read your Granola meeting notes, through Granola's own service. Ask it things like:

- "What did we agree with Acme on Tuesday?"
- "Which action items did I take on this week?"
- "Get me ready for my call with Sara: what did we talk about last time?"

Activating it signs the bot in: you approve it on
Granola's own page. On Granola's free plan the bot reaches your notes from the last 30 days,
without transcripts.

The bot only reads: nothing in Granola is changed.
