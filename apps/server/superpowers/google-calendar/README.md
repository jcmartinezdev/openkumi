Lets a bot work in your Google Calendar, through Google's own service. Ask it things like:

- "What does my week look like, and where is there room for a long focus block?"
- "Find an hour next week that works for me and Ana, and draft the invitation."
- "Put the dentist on Thursday at 9, with a reminder the day before."

The bot reads freely and adds events to your calendar. It asks before it invites anyone,
moves or cancels an event, or answers an invitation, since those send mail to other people.

Open Bot has to be registered with Google once, for all the Google superpowers; this page walks
you through it. After that, activate it for a bot and sign in to Google in the bot's browser,
with the account that bot should use.
