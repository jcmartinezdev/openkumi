Lets a bot keep track of your competitors. Ask it things like:

- "Who are we really up against, and how do they price?"
- "What do people complain about in Acme's reviews?"
- "Compare us with the top three, on what our customers care about."
- "Tell me when any of them changes its pricing."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and ask.

The bot keeps a dated profile of each competitor in its files, under `marketing/competitors/`,
with a link for everything in it. With **Watch a page** active too, it can watch their pricing
pages and changelogs for you; without it, it checks them once a month.

It uses public information only: it never signs up under a false name or poses as a customer.
