Lets a bot keep your contacts in folk, through folk's own service. Ask it things like:

- "Who did I meet at the conference last week? Add them to the Investors group."
- "Remind me to follow up with everyone I haven't talked to in a month."
- "Draft a short check-in campaign for the Customers group."

Activating it signs the bot in: you approve it on folk's
own page. It works on every folk plan, and the bot sees what your folk account sees.

The bot reads freely and can add contacts, notes and tasks, and draft campaigns. It asks before
it changes a contact, enriches one (which spends folk credits) or sends a campaign: sent email
cannot be taken back.
