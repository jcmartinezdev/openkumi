Gives a bot exchange rates for over 300 currencies, from two free services. Ask it things like:

- "How much is 250 pounds in yen?"
- "What was the dollar worth in euros on 2 March 2020?"
- "Show me the euro against the Swiss franc over the last year."
- "Tell me when the dollar goes above 1.15 euros."

There is nothing to set up and nothing to sign in to. Activate it for a bot and ask.

The 30 major currencies come from the European Central Bank's reference rates, through
[Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev/). The rest, crypto included, come from the free
[Currency API](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api), once a day.

These are reference rates. A bank, card or exchange office adds its own margin, so what you pay
is a little worse; the bot says so when you are about to pay or change money.
