Lets a bot work in your Google Docs, through Google's own service. Ask it things like:

- "Read the project brief and list the open questions." (with a link to it)
- "Add a summary of today's call at the end of the meeting notes."
- "Tighten the introduction of this draft." (with a link to it)

The bot reads and adds to the documents you point it to. It asks before it rewrites or deletes
anything already there. To find documents by name, also activate Google Drive for the bot;
otherwise give it a link.

Open Bot has to be registered with Google once, for all the Google superpowers; this page walks
you through it. After that, activate it for a bot and sign in to Google in the bot's browser,
with the account that bot should use.
