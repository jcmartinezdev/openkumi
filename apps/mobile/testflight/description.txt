OpenKumi is a free, open-source crew of AI bots that live on your own computer, each with its own memory, its own signed-in browser and only the powers you give it. They run on the Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan you already have.

This is the iPhone app. It talks to OpenKumi on your computer, so you need that first: get the desktop app for Mac, Windows or Linux from openkumi.ai, then pair this phone by scanning the code it shows. At home the phone reaches your computer over Wi-Fi; away from home, over Tailscale.

From the phone you can chat with your bots, send them photos and files, dictate, call a bot and talk with it, watch or take over a bot's browser, and set up automations.

OpenKumi is in alpha: it changes from one version to the next, and some things will not work yet. Tell us what breaks: take a screenshot to send feedback through TestFlight, or open an issue at github.com/jcmartinezdev/openkumi/issues.
