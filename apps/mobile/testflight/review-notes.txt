OpenKumi's iPhone app is the remote for OpenKumi, a free, open-source program that runs AI bots on the user's own computer. There is no account and no server of ours: everything the app shows comes from the user's own computer, which it reaches on the home network (or over Tailscale). So no sign-in is needed, but the app needs a computer running OpenKumi to pair with.

To try it:
1. On a Mac, download the desktop app from https://openkumi.ai (Download) and open it. Choose "Run them on this computer". A bot answers through an AI program the person already has; the app shows how to sign in to Claude Code, Codex (ChatGPT) or Grok.
2. In the desktop app: Settings > Profile & devices > "Let other devices connect", then "Pair a device". It shows a QR code and a code.
3. On the iPhone, on the same Wi-Fi: tap "Pair with your computer" on the welcome screen and scan the QR code.
The list of bots then appears. You can chat with a bot, send it photos, dictate, call it, and watch its browser.

Permissions: Local Network (to reach the person's computer), Camera (to scan the pairing code and take photos to send), Microphone (dictation, which is written down on the iPhone itself, and calls with a bot), Photos (to choose photos to send). Background audio keeps a call going while the screen is locked.

Usage and error reports are only sent if the person agrees, and the computer sends them, not the phone. The app is an alpha and says so on its welcome screen; it is meant for TestFlight now and the App Store later.

The source code is public: https://github.com/jcmartinezdev/openkumi
